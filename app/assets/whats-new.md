# What's new
- **Agentic Digital Mixer:** A full Mixer view with live, read-only X32/M32 metering and channel-strip state. Turn it on with `SOUND_BUDDY_FEATURES=console,mixer`. Apply to Board and Undo stay visible but locked in this build.
- **Optional Local AI and Cloud AI:** Off-by-default Settings toggles. Cloud AI uses your own installed `claude` CLI and stores the token in the Mac keychain.
